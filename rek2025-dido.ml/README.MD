# Scam Report: Odido Smishing Scam – rek2025-dido.ml

**Scam Date**: November 2025  
**Status**: Resolved 

---

## Scam Summary
This scam targets Odido (formerly T-Mobile Netherlands) customers via SMS messages claiming that there is an unpaid invoice requiring immediate payment to avoid SIM card suspension. Victims are urged to visit a fraudulent payment website.

> **Original message (Dutch)**:  
> "Vergeet je ons niet? Er staat nog een rekening open. Betaal voor 24/11 via https://rek2025-dido.ml, anders blokkeren we je simkaart. Tot snel, Odido."

> **English translation**:  
> "Don’t forget us? There’s still an outstanding bill. Pay before 24/11 via https://rek2025-dido.ml, or we’ll block your SIM card. See you soon, Odido."

When opened on a **mobile browser**, the link redirects to a phishing website mimicking Odido’s platform. On **desktop browsers**, visitors are redirected to Google.com — a method used to evade automated scanners or analysts.  

The site uses a **fake chat function** that invites victims to request a “payment link,” through which their payment details are harvested.

---

## Source Analysis
- **Impersonated Entity**: Odido (Dutch telecommunications provider)  
- **Scammer's Contact Method**: SMS (smishing)  
- **Website Used**: rek2025-dido.ml  
- **Registrar**: Gransy.com  
- **Hosting Provider**: GLOBAL CONNECTIVITY SOLUTIONS LLP (IP: 147.45.177.180)  
- **TLS Provider**: Let’s Encrypt  
- **Chat Service**: Tidio (Project ID: fecnuhvn59dunyqhwn2lvcbo4tutmtmj)  
- **Cloud Service Provider**: Not using Cloudflare

---

## Scam Tactics
### 1. Impersonation of a Known Brand
The scammers copy Odido’s communication tone by sending “billing reminder” messages to establish trust.

### 2. Fear and Urgency
Users are pressed to act quickly to avoid SIM card suspension.

### 3. Device-Specific Redirection
Desktop users are redirected to Google.com, while mobile users reach the phishing domain.

### 4. Fraudulent Chat Function
Using a legitimate chat integration (Tidio), scammers issue fake “payment links.”

---

## Actions Taken
- Prepared takedown request to Gransy.com.  
- Prepared abuse report to GLOBAL CONNECTIVITY SOLUTIONS LLP.  
- Prepared takedown request to Tidio.co.  
- Odido’s fraud department will be notified.  
- Reports prepared for Fraudehelpdesk.nl and Veilig Internetten.nl.

- Tidio took immediate action after the scam was reported.
- The hoster seems to have changed their contact information.
- The website is down. 

---


## Lessons & Takeaways
### 1. Red Flags
- Non-brand or unknown domains (.ml).  
- Payment requests by SMS.  
- Links behaving differently on mobile vs desktop.

### 2. Stay Safe
- Check all invoices through official channels.  
- Never click unexpected links.  
- If personal data shared, notify bank and Odido immediately.
